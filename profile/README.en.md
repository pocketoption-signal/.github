## About

PocketOption Signal helps you analyze the chart right inside the [Pocket Option](https://pocketoption.com) terminal. Pick indicators or a ready-made preset, and the tool watches live prices and tells you when to open a **CALL** or a **PUT**. It works with forex, crypto, commodities, stocks and indices, on both demo and real accounts.

The project is **completely free**: no affiliate links, no subscription, no payments.

## Features

- **Signals.** Analysis tuned to your expiration from 3 seconds to 5 minutes, 10 configurable indicators, "all filters", "any one" and "context" (trend + entry timing) modes, ready-made presets, sound alerts.
- **Auto-trading.** Clicks CALL or PUT on a signal. Amount, expiration and asset are synced with the platform. Shows account type and balance. Take Profit and Take Loss.
- **Money management.** 8 stake-sizing systems, hints on when it is safe to raise the stake, win/loss streak tracking.
- **Journal.** Signal, win and loss stats, trade history with CSV export, per-asset and per-indicator stats.
- **Convenience.** Panel right on the site, asset follows the chart, settings export/import, Russian and English UI.

## Getting started

1. Install the [extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kmikglaokkegcnmiidoboodmbeihjofe) and open the terminal on pocketoption.com.
2. Switch to a **demo account** and pick an asset.
3. Open the PocketOption Signal panel, choose a preset or your own indicators, amount and expiration.
4. Click "Start analysis". Turn on auto-trading if you like.
5. Move to a real account only after practicing on demo.
